CCTV Installation & Maintenance

We provide professional CCTV installation, configuration, and maintenance for homes, offices, and commercial spaces. Our team sets up cameras and recording systems for reliable video coverage based on your premises and security requirements.

NVR / DVR Recording Systems

We install and configure NVR and DVR recording systems, including camera connectivity, storage setup, recording configuration, and access to recorded footage. We also provide troubleshooting and maintenance for supported systems.

HD & IP Camera Installation

We install and configure HD, IP, and analog cameras with practical placement and system setup to provide the coverage your premises require. We can also support upgrades, replacement of equipment, and ongoing maintenance.

Remote & Mobile Monitoring

We configure supported CCTV systems for remote viewing so you can access live camera feeds and recorded footage from compatible devices. Our work focuses on system setup and maintenance; we do not provide a separate surveillance or monitoring service.

Biometric Attendance Management

Implement accurate and secure workforce attendance systems that support real-time tracking, employee records, automated reporting, and stronger protection against proxy attendance.

eSSL Device & Application Integration

Set up and configure eSSL biometric devices with desktop and web applications, live server connectivity, and data synchronization for practical attendance monitoring and management.

Fingerprint, Face & RFID Systems

Choose from fingerprint, face recognition, and RFID-based attendance or access systems designed to provide accurate identification and more controlled entry management.

Ongoing Maintenance & Support

Keep surveillance and biometric systems operating reliably with ongoing maintenance, support, monitoring assistance, and upgrades for your security and access-control environment.
